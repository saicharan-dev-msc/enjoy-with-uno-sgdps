About

Enjoy with Uno is a fan-made card game that follows the standard rules of the classic matching game. UNO is a trademark of Mattel, and this site is not affiliated with or endorsed by Mattel.

How to play

  • Each player starts with 7 cards. Match the top card by color, number or symbol.
  • Skip jumps the next player. Reverse flips direction (with two players it works like a Skip). Draw Two makes the next player draw 2 and lose the turn.
  • Wild lets you pick the color. Wild Draw Four does that and makes the next player draw 4, but you can only play it when you hold no card of the current color.
  • No playable card? Draw one. If it fits, play it or keep it. If not, the turn passes.
  • Play down to one card and tap UNO! within 3 seconds, or you draw 2.
  • First player to empty their hand wins the round. Wins are counted until you change the setup.
  • Optional setting: "Let players pass the penalty on". If someone makes you draw, you can play your own Draw Two or Draw Four instead, and the next player draws the total.
  • Online with friends: one person creates a room and shares its 5-character code. Friends open this website on their own phones, choose Online with friends and enter the code. Empty seats are played by the computer.
  • Your profile is saved in this browser and opens automatically next time. Every round you win adds one gold star, and the total shows at the top right. Log out (top right) erases the profile and its stars.
  • With two or more humans, the screen is covered between turns. Pass the device, then tap to reveal your cards.
